# 🇬🇧 English

## 📖 Overview

**CONFIGFARS Horror Game** is the newest game from the **CONFIGFARS Team**, blending tense horror atmosphere with fast-paced action. Explore dark environments, uncover what lurks in the shadows, and fight to stay alive. The game runs directly in your browser, so there is nothing to download or install.

> ⚠️ **The game is currently in Beta.** You may run into bugs, unfinished content, or balance issues. Your feedback helps us improve it.

## ✨ Features

- 😱 **Immersive horror atmosphere** — dark visuals, tense pacing, and unsettling surprises.
- ⚔️ **Action-driven gameplay** — don't just hide; fight back and survive.
- 🌐 **Instant play** — launch directly from your browser, no installation required.
- 🆓 **Free to play** — enjoy the full beta at no cost.
- 🔓 **Open source** — released under the permissive MIT License.
- 🔄 **Frequent updates** — new content and fixes are added throughout the beta.

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Open **<https://horror-game.configfars.ir/>** in a modern browser.
2. Allow the page to load completely.
3. Start the game and follow the on-screen instructions.
4. Use headphones for the best experience. 🎧

### Default Controls *(may change during beta)*

|---|---|
| Move | `W` `A` `S` `D` |
| Look around | Mouse |

- Recommended: a desktop or laptop and headphones
